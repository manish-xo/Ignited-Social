How the Brand Manager cards work

6 managers, one card each. Visitors browse them with arrows. The “Today’s workflow” list on each card is picked from the owner’s copy and changes once a day at 12:00 PM India time. Names, photos and experience are placeholders until we get the real ones.

6 managers, arrows to browse

One card per real Brand Manager. Left / right arrows move between them.

2

Manager 1 gets a special card

The first manager visitors see uses 1 of the 6 “special” lists, written with “your”.

3

Managers 2–6 share the pool

5 of the 15 pool lists are picked, one per manager. No two match on the same day.

4

New picks daily at 12 PM IST

Everyone sees the same picks until 12:00 PM IST, then a fresh set is chosen.

Pick any date and time (India time) and watch the cards switch at 12 PM.
