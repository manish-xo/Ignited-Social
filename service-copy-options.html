What's wrong with the current copy

  • Most sections stack an eyebrow, a two-part heading, a subheading and extra small print. That's 4–7 lines of text before the content starts.
  • The hero says the same thing twice: the subheading and the “We grow your audience / You create and publish” lines.
  • “Brand Manager” appears 20+ times, and “7 days free” in four places.
  • Pricing explains the refund twice, once in each plan card and again below them.
  • Two-part headings (“X. And Y.”) are used in five sections, so none of them stands out.
  • A few typos and mismatches. These are flagged in the section where they occur.

Rules used for every option

  • One heading and at most one supporting line.
  • Eyebrows kept only where they add information.
  • Every fact comes from the current site: prices, timings, plans, refund. Nothing new is invented.
  • Customer quotes are left exactly as written.
